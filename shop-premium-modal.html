About the Fabric

What is Modal?

Modal is a semi-synthetic fibre made from beech tree pulp, known for its exceptional softness, breathability, and ability to hold colour beautifully. It is 50% more moisture-absorbent than cotton, making it ideal for the Pakistani climate.

At Nurah, we use a premium modal blend that is wrinkle-resistant, colour-fast, and gentle on the skin. Our modal sets drape with an effortless fluidity that feels luxurious without being heavy — the perfect foundation for modest dressing done with intention.
